Information the Rider app handles

  • Account and contact details: your name, mobile number, email address, address details, account and security records, and information needed to verify your rider account. Supabase Auth processes sign-in credentials; LahuzGo does not display or store your password in the rider profile.
  • Rider application and documents: rider application details, driver's license number and expiry date, vehicle model, plate number, vehicle color, vehicle and license photos, profile photos, application status, approval notes, and related administrator review records.
  • Delivery and availability information: your online availability, assigned booking details, pickup and destination information, recipient contact details, delivery instructions, booking status, customer messages, ratings or feedback, earnings and remittance records, and support conversations needed to complete a delivery.
  • Device and local app information: notification settings and tokens, app preferences, temporary navigation or notification data, selected photos or videos, and files cached in the app's private storage. The app does not upload your entire photo library.

Location and map services

When you are using Rider features that need navigation or delivery coordination, the app may handle precise device location, route progress, heading, and coordinates associated with the assigned delivery. When you are marked available online, the app may periodically sync your current location so the operations system can coordinate requests and show the rider position needed for an active delivery.

The Android app requests precise foreground location permission. It does not request Android background-location permission. Location access can be disabled in Android settings, but delivery assignment, navigation, or availability features may then be limited.

Mapbox provides maps, place search, reverse geocoding, and route services. Mapbox may process the coordinates and route information needed to provide those services under its own privacy practices.

How information is used

We use rider information to create and secure your account, verify your mobile number, review rider applications and documents, approve or restrict rider access, match riders with delivery requests, coordinate pickup and drop-off, provide navigation and location updates, send notifications, calculate or record earnings and remittances, provide support, prevent misuse, protect customers and riders, and maintain the service.

Who receives information

Information needed to complete a delivery may be available to authorized LahuzGo operations or support staff, the customer connected to that delivery, and the rider assigned to the request. This can include pickup and destination details, recipient contact details, item descriptions, delivery instructions, relevant messages, and location or status updates. Rider applications and identity or vehicle documents are intended for authorized LahuzGo review and are not made public.

We use service providers for specific functions: Supabase for authentication, database, private file storage, and realtime app features; Mapbox for maps, geocoding, and routes; Semaphore or another SMS service for verification messages when enabled; and local Android notification services for device notifications. These providers process information needed for their services under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information.

Photos and local app files

You choose whether to capture or select rider application documents, vehicle photos, profile photos, or chat attachments. The app uses camera or photo access only for the feature you choose. Selected files may be uploaded to private LahuzGo storage for rider review, delivery support, or communication, and some files may remain cached in the app's private storage on your phone.

Remote deletion cannot erase files already cached on your device. Clear LahuzGo Rider's app storage or uninstall the app to remove local files from your phone.

Retention and rider account deletion

We keep rider account, application, delivery, location, support, earnings, and document information while needed to operate the service, review safety and approval matters, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations. To request deletion, use the in-app account deletion option when available or follow the Rider account deletion instructions.

Some records may need to be retained for a reasonable period for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, payment, dispute-resolution, or regulatory reasons. Provider backups may expire on the provider's own schedule. Deletion may be delayed while an active delivery, investigation, remittance, or dispute must be completed.

Security

We use authentication, access controls, private storage, secure network connections, and role-based administrator access to help protect rider information. No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and verification codes private and report suspected account misuse promptly.
