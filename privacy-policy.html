Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the LahuzGo Customer Android app handles information for Padala and Pabili delivery requests. LahuzGo is operated with Lahuz Delivery Service in Ormoc City, Leyte, Philippines. This page is for the Customer app, not the separate Rider or Admin apps.

Information the app handles

  • Account and contact details: your name, mobile number, contact email, chosen profile photo, and account/security records. Supabase Auth processes sign-in credentials; LahuzGo does not store your password in the customer profile table.
  • Delivery requests: Padala or Pabili details, one or more pickup names and addresses, landmarks and map pins, destination details, recipient name and phone number, item descriptions and quantities, delivery instructions, booking status, and related service/fee records.
  • Messages and support: text you send in a booking conversation; photos or videos you choose to attach; feedback messages, contact email, and screenshots you choose to submit to support.
  • Notifications and device data: a push-notification token and platform information needed to send booking, chat, and account notices, plus notification preferences.

Location and map searches

When you use the map, choose your current location, search for a place, or request a route, the app handles the search text and/or coordinates needed for that feature. Android location access is requested only while using the app; the Customer app does not request background location access. A pin you submit becomes part of your delivery request.

Mapbox provides maps, place search/geocoding, and route services. Temporary map search results and route estimates are not saved in LahuzGo's booking database.

How information is used

We use these details to create and protect your account, verify your mobile number, save addresses, submit and coordinate deliveries, match requests with riders, show delivery progress, communicate with you, provide customer support, send notifications, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

Who receives information

Information needed to complete a request is available to the LahuzGo operations/support team and the rider assigned to that request. This can include pickup and destination details, recipient contact details, item descriptions, instructions, and relevant conversation messages. We do not make customer account or booking data public.

We use service providers for specific functions: Supabase for authentication, database, private file storage, and realtime app features; Mapbox for maps, place search/geocoding, and route services; Semaphore for SMS verification messages; and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notifications. These providers process the information needed for their services under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information.

Photos and local app files

You choose whether to add a profile photo, chat attachment, or support screenshot. The app does not upload your entire photo library. Selected profile photos, chat media, and support screenshots are stored in private app storage. Chat messages and some media may also be cached in the app's private files on your phone for offline use.

Remote account deletion cannot erase files already cached on your device. Clear LahuzGo's app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local files.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and request information while needed to provide the service, maintain safety, resolve support issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. To delete your account, use Account → Delete account in the app, or follow the external account deletion instructions.

Deletion timing: in-app requests have a 7-day grace period. Signing in during those 7 days cancels the request. Active Padala or Pabili deliveries must be finished or cancelled before deletion can proceed. Scheduled cleanup removes the account and associated customer profile, saved addresses, delivery/conversation history, messages, uploaded account files, and push token when no active operation remains. Cleanup runs periodically, so it may happen after—not exactly at—the 7-day point.

Limited records may need to be retained where required for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons. Provider backups may expire on the provider's own backup schedule. You may separately need to clear local app files from your phone.

Security

We use authentication, access controls, private storage, and secure network connections to help protect information. No internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.
