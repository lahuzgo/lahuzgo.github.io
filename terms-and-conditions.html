3. Padala and Pabili requests

Padala: use this service for an item that is already purchased, paid for, prepared, and ready for pickup. Submitting the request is not itself proof of payment or a rider assignment.

Pabili: list the items and stores you want the rider to visit and provide accurate instructions. Review the confirmed details and accept any fee shown in the app before the request proceeds to rider assignment.

A submitted request is a request for service, not a guarantee that a rider is available or that a store has an item in stock. The app or support will show updates when a request is reviewed, confirmed, assigned, changed, or cancelled.

4. Fees, prices, and payment

The delivery fee shown while preparing a request is an estimate based on the route and selected stops. The final delivery fee, if different, is set and shown for review in the app. For Pabili, item availability and prices are confirmed through support; do not assume an unconfirmed item price or estimate is final.

LahuzGo supports GCash, cash, or bank transfer, depending on the booking. Review the total amount and the payment instructions shown in the app or confirmed by LahuzGo before paying. Use only the payment details confirmed for your booking. Do not send your password, one-time code, or full payment-card credentials in chat.

If a Padala item delivered to the recipient or a Pabili item bought by the rider does not match what was confirmed in the booking, the customer may contact LahuzGo support and request a refund covering the affected item and the delivery fee. LahuzGo will review the booking to confirm the mismatch and the refund amount and payment arrangements. Refunds are not automatic for unrelated cancellations or other issues; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

5. Changes and cancellations

Check all stops, item details, recipient information, and pins before submitting. You may cancel directly while no rider has been assigned. Once a rider is assigned, submit a cancellation request and wait for Admin to review it. The booking is not cancelled unless and until Admin approves the request and its status changes to cancelled.

Contact support through the request conversation if you need to correct details or explain a problem. Changes may not be possible after a rider has started or completed part of the request. Any applicable charges or refund will be handled according to the facts of the request and applicable law.
