3. Pa-angkas, Padala and Pabili requests

Pa-angkas: choose one pickup point and one destination for your motorcycle ride. You can use your current location or a map pin for pickup, then search for or pin your destination. Add any optional instructions for your rider, review the fare and route, and submit. Submitting starts the rider search; it does not mean a rider has accepted or that payment has been made. Check the assigned rider and booking details before starting your trip.

Padala: use this service for an item that is already purchased, paid for, prepared, and ready for pickup. Submitting the request is not itself proof of payment or a rider assignment.

Pabili: list the items and stores you want the rider to visit and provide accurate instructions. Store stock, item prices, substitutions, and the delivery fee may need to be confirmed with support in the request conversation. Review the confirmed details and accept any fee shown in the app before the request proceeds to rider assignment.

A submitted request is a request for service, not a guarantee that a rider is available or that a store has an item in stock. The app or support will show updates when a request is reviewed, confirmed, assigned, changed, or cancelled.

4. Fees, prices, and payment

Pa-angkas fare: the app shows the fare for your pickup-to-destination route before you submit. Pa-angkas has its own rates set by LahuzGo, separate from Padala and Pabili delivery fees. If a quote expires or the fare changes before submission, review the refreshed amount before submitting again. Estimated travel time covers the route from pickup; finding a rider and waiting at pickup take additional time.

The delivery fee shown while preparing a request is an estimate based on the route and selected stops. The final delivery fee, if different, is set and shown for review in the app. For Pabili, item availability and prices are confirmed through support; do not assume an unconfirmed item price or estimate is final.

Payment arrangements depend on the booking. For Padala and Pabili, LahuzGo may support GCash, cash or bank transfer as confirmed for that booking. The Pa-angkas request form does not include a payment-method selection. Review the fare or total and follow the payment instructions shown in the app or confirmed by LahuzGo. A booking submission or fare quote is not a payment receipt. Do not send your password, one-time code, or full payment-card credentials in chat.

If a Padala item delivered to the recipient or a Pabili item bought by the rider does not match what was confirmed in the booking, the customer may contact LahuzGo support and request a refund covering the affected item and the delivery fee. LahuzGo will review the booking to confirm the mismatch and the refund amount and payment arrangements. Refunds are not automatic for unrelated cancellations or other issues; applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

5. Changes and cancellations

Check pickup and destination pins, instructions, item details and contact information before submitting. During rider search, the app shows “Waiting for a rider” for the first 10 minutes, then “Still looking for a rider” until 20 minutes. If nobody has accepted after 20 minutes, it shows “No rider has accepted yet” and offers the choice to keep waiting or cancel and try again later. The direct Cancel booking button appears at that stage while the request remains open and no rider has accepted.

Once a rider has accepted, use Request cancellation and wait for Admin to review it. A pending request does not cancel the booking; check that its status changes to cancelled. Completed or already closed bookings cannot be cancelled. Contact support if you believe a trip or delivery was marked completed incorrectly.

Contact support through the request conversation if you need to correct details or explain a problem. Changes may not be possible after a rider has started or completed part of the request. Any applicable charges or refund will be handled according to the facts of the request and applicable law.

6. Passenger safety, items and packaging

For Pa-angkas, meet your assigned rider at a safe pickup point, check that the booking is yours, and wear a properly fitted and securely fastened helmet. Follow safe riding instructions and do not begin or continue a ride if conditions are unsafe. Only start the trip after the passenger is on board. The rider confirms trip completion after the passenger has reached the destination; the app then shows the completed booking. Contact support about a safety concern or incorrect status when it is safe to do so.

Only request items that are lawful to buy, possess, and transport, and that a rider can safely carry. Do not ask a rider to handle illegal, dangerous, restricted, or otherwise prohibited items. LahuzGo may decline or stop a request that appears unlawful or unsafe.

For Padala, package the item securely and describe any handling needs accurately. You are responsible for the item details and for making sure the pickup person and recipient are ready at the stated locations.

7. Your responsibilities during a request

Choose the correct map pins and provide useful address details or landmarks. Keep your phone reachable and respond to reasonable questions from support or the rider. For Pa-angkas, be ready at your pickup point; for Padala and Pabili, make sure the pickup contact and recipient can hand over or receive the items. Incorrect addresses, unavailable contacts, unsafe conditions, traffic, weather, store availability, and other circumstances may delay or prevent completion.

Route, distance, and arrival-time information in the app are estimates and may change. Do not rely on an estimated arrival time as a guarantee.
