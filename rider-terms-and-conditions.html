1. Agreement and eligibility

By creating a rider account, submitting a rider application, or using the Rider app after approval, you agree to these terms and the Rider Privacy Policy. You must be legally able to perform the ride or delivery services you accept in your service area and must provide documents and information that belong to you and are current and accurate.

If you do not agree to these terms, do not create or use a rider account. These terms do not remove any rights or remedies that cannot legally be limited.

2. Your rider account and application

  • Provide truthful, complete, and current name, contact, address, vehicle, license, and application information.
  • Use only your own identity, driver's license, vehicle information, photos, and documents. Do not impersonate another person or share your account.
  • Keep your password and one-time verification codes private and tell LahuzGo promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.
  • LahuzGo may review applications and documents before approving rider access. Application submission does not guarantee approval, assignments, earnings, or continued access.

3. Ride and delivery work and availability

  • Keep your availability status accurate and accept only requests you can complete safely and lawfully.
  • Review the service type, pickup, destination, passenger or recipient details, fare or delivery fee, route and instructions before accepting or starting a booking. Ask support when details are unclear.
  • Protect passenger, recipient and customer information, handle customer items carefully, and update ride or delivery status honestly and promptly.
  • Routes, distances, and arrival times are estimates. Traffic, weather, road conditions, customer availability, store availability, and other events may delay or prevent completion.
  • Earnings, remittance, cancellation, and refund handling follow the instructions and records shown in the app or provided by authorized LahuzGo support. Do not promise a refund or change a booking outside the authorized process.
Pa-angkas workflow: accept an offered ride only when you can serve it safely. Meet the passenger at the booking's pickup, confirm passenger pickup, and start the trip only when the passenger is on board. After reaching the destination, use the trip-completion action and confirm that the passenger has arrived. The app requires the pickup and trip-start stages before completion. Pa-angkas completion does not require a delivery-proof photo or a separate customer receipt-confirmation step.

A completion action saved while disconnected remains pending until it syncs and the booking shows as completed. Check the booking status after reconnecting. Cancellation is unavailable once completion is pending or the booking is completed. If you need to cancel an accepted booking, use the cancellation-request process and wait for Admin's decision; a pending request does not cancel the booking. Contact support if a completed trip or delivery needs investigation.

4. Safety, lawful use, and conduct

Follow applicable traffic, transport, safety, consumer-protection, and other laws. For Pa-angkas, confirm that you are meeting the correct passenger and that both rider and passenger have properly fitted, securely fastened helmets before starting. Do not start or continue a trip when conditions are unsafe. Do not carry illegal, dangerous, restricted, or unsafe items. Do not drive while impaired, use the app in a dangerous way, harass or threaten a customer, expose private customer information, manipulate location or booking status, or interfere with the service.

Use reasonable care for your own safety and the safety of customers, recipients, and other road users. Contact the appropriate emergency service first when there is an immediate emergency, then notify LahuzGo support when safe.

5. Earnings, records, and communications

Review the Pa-angkas trip fare or Padala/Pabili delivery fee shown for the booking. Pa-angkas uses its own fare rates, separate from delivery fees. Keep accurate records and follow the earnings-share and remittance settings and booking amounts shown in the app or confirmed by authorized LahuzGo support. Do not change the recorded fare, promise an unauthorized refund, or ask for a customer's passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details. LahuzGo may review trip, delivery, communication, location, earnings and remittance records to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and protect users.

LahuzGo may reject an application, pause or suspend an account, remove a rider from a request, or end rider access when information is inaccurate, documents are invalid or expired, safety or service rules are breached, the service is misused, or action is reasonably needed to protect users or comply with law. Where appropriate, LahuzGo may review the matter and request clarification or updated documents.

You may stop using the Rider app and request account deletion through the Rider account deletion instructions. Open Pa-angkas rides, Padala or Pabili bookings, remittances, disputes, investigations, or legal obligations may delay deletion or require limited records to be retained.
