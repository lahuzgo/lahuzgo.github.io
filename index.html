Let’s talk ↗

What’s the difference between Padala and Pabili?

Padala is for sending an item from your pickup location to a recipient. Pabili is for asking a rider to buy the items you specify and bring them to you. Both start in the LahuzGo customer app.

How do I make a request?

Download the customer app on Android, create or sign in to your account, and select Padala or Pabili. Add the locations and required details, then review your request before submitting it. Requests are made in the app, not on this website.

Can I follow my rider’s progress?

Once your rider is assigned, you can follow request status and available rider-location updates in the app. You can also use the request’s chat to stay connected. Location updates depend on the rider’s connection and location sharing.

Is LahuzGo available on iPhone?

The customer app is available for Android. The iOS app is coming soon. For questions about availability, contact us at atonlopez55@gmail.com.
