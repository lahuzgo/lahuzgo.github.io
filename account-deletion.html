Option 1: Request deletion in the app

  1. Open LahuzGo and go to your Account screen.
  2. Tap Delete account, complete the confirmation steps, and submit the request.
  3. Sign out and do not sign in again during the 7-day grace period. Signing in during that period cancels the request.
Active delivery: an active Padala or Pabili delivery must be finished or cancelled before deletion can proceed.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email atonlopez55@gmail.com with the subject LahuzGo Customer account deletion request. Include the name and mobile number or email address on the account so we can verify which account you own.

Do not send your password or one-time verification code. We may contact you to verify the request or resolve an active delivery before deletion can proceed. After we confirm the request, deletion is normally completed within 7 days, subject to active deliveries and legal or safety requirements.

What happens to your information

After the in-app grace period, scheduled cleanup removes the account and associated customer profile, saved addresses, delivery/conversation history, messages, uploaded account files, and push-notification token when no active operation remains. The cleanup runs periodically, so deletion may happen after the grace period rather than at an exact time.

Limited information may be retained where required for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons. Provider backups may expire according to their own schedules. Account deletion removes data from active LahuzGo systems but cannot erase information independently held by third-party providers or files already stored on your phone.

Chat messages and some media may be cached in LahuzGo's private files on your phone. Clear the app storage or uninstall LahuzGo to remove those local files.
