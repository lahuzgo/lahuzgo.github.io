Option 1: Request deletion in the Rider app

  1. Open LahuzGo Rider and sign in.
  2. Open the Rider menu and choose Delete account.
  3. Complete the confirmation steps and submit the request.
  4. Follow the confirmation shown in the app. The in-app request has a 7-day grace period; sign out and do not sign in again during that period. Signing in again can cancel the pending deletion request.
Open bookings: finish or cancel every assigned Pa-angkas ride, Padala delivery or Pabili booking still waiting or in progress before requesting deletion. A cancellation awaiting Admin approval is still an open booking. A pending remittance, unresolved safety matter or open dispute may also need review. Contact support if you cannot resolve a booking or complete the request.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the Rider app, email atonlopez55@gmail.com with the subject LahuzGo Rider account deletion request. Include the name and mobile number or email address on the rider account so we can identify and verify the account.

Do not send your password, one-time verification code, driver's license number, or document photos in the first email. We may contact you through a reasonable verification step before acting on the request.

What happens to your information

After verification, the applicable grace period and resolution of open bookings, remittances, disputes or safety and legal matters, we will remove or de-identify rider account information and associated rider profile, application details, uploaded documents, saved vehicle information, Pa-angkas trip and Padala/Pabili booking records, messages, and notification information from active LahuzGo systems where deletion is legally and operationally possible. Related booking data can include pickup and destination pins, trip instructions, status updates and fare or earnings records. Processing and scheduled cleanup may occur after the grace period rather than at an exact time.

Limited information may be retained where required for legal, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory reasons. Provider backups may expire according to their own schedules. Account deletion cannot erase files already cached in the app's private storage on your phone; clear the app storage or uninstall LahuzGo Rider to remove local files.
