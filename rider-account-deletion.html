Option 1: Request deletion in the Rider app

  1. Open LahuzGo Rider and sign in.
  2. Open your account or security settings and choose the account deletion option when available.
  3. Complete the confirmation steps and submit the request.
  4. Keep the app closed or signed out if the app shows a grace period or asks you to confirm the request.
An active delivery, pending remittance, unresolved safety matter, or open dispute may need to be completed or reviewed before deletion can proceed.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the Rider app, email atonlopez55@gmail.com with the subject LahuzGo Rider account deletion request. Include the name and mobile number or email address on the rider account so we can identify and verify the account.

Do not send your password, one-time verification code, driver's license number, or document photos in the first email. We may contact you through a reasonable verification step before acting on the request.

What happens to your information

After verification and completion of any active delivery, remittance, dispute, safety, or legal process, we will remove or de-identify the rider account information and associated rider profile, application details, uploaded documents, saved vehicle information, delivery-related records, messages, and notification information from active LahuzGo systems where deletion is legally and operationally possible.

Limited information may be retained where required for legal, accounting, safety, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory reasons. Provider backups may expire according to their own schedules. Account deletion cannot erase files already cached in the app's private storage on your phone; clear the app storage or uninstall LahuzGo Rider to remove local files.
